What a Session Holds

Hello, I’m so glad you’re here.

Change rarely arrives gently. More often it moves through us like weather, unsettling what was familiar and asking us to listen more closely, and so often the body feels it first, long before the mind has found the words.

Each session is a dedicated space to slow down and listen. Together we tend to the inner vessel, strengthening it so you can hold your life with greater steadiness and agency. We invite clarity, attend to what feels energetically stuck, and gently explore the unconscious patterns that keep returning.

Whether you arrive in the midst of a difficult season, longing to understand the patterns that shape your days, or carrying a quiet knowing that something more is possible, I have the tools to support you.

Each session is uniquely shaped around where you are in the moment, as I listen for what will best support you. This is an integrative, holistic exploration of Self, a remembrance of body, mind and spirit as one living whole.

I invite you to explore your connection to the sacred, to consciousness, to the deep well of love that exists in your heart.

About Julia

My path has been shaped by both science and the sacred.

I hold a degree in science and have studied psychology, pharmacology, nutrition and trauma-informed approaches to well-being, and I’ve spent more than two decades in the fast-paced world of health care as a pharmacist. Alongside that work, I have been blessed to learn from beloved mentors, among them physicians, shamans and energy healers, and have devoted myself to the study of ancient psycho-spiritual wisdom, mindfulness and personal development.

My trauma-informed counseling practice draws from many modern and ancient traditions. My deepest hope is that you leave each session more grounded and more spacious within yourself, able to meet your life with tenderness, grace and intention.

Our current times are calling many of us to turn inward and reconnect with our spiritual core.

The Tools

These are the psycho-spiritual tools and practices I bring into my holistic coaching.

Guidance and coaching

A gentle yet transformative process that helps you reconnect with your inner wisdom, align with your purpose, and create real change in your life. Honoring the mind-body-spirit connection, I blend spiritual practices with practical tools to help you create the meaningful life you know is possible. It’s an invitation to explore your path with compassion and curiosity, nurture your intuition, and take intentional steps forward—so you can move through life feeling more inspired and grounded.

Relationship coaching

I invite you to meet and explore your emotions with me in a safe space.

From an emotional and psychological standpoint, shame or repression is a learned behavior. It creates a fracture within our psyche, and blocks our creative life force energy. It is then playing out unconsciously in every other area of our lives.

What is the deeper pattern beneath the symptom? What happens when we bring that pattern into the light?

We are living in a heightened crisis of intimacy. Regardless of how we got here, I invite you to participate in the evolutionary story — healing relationships with ourselves, with each other, with the mystery of life, and with the divine.

There is an inner calling, knowing that something different is possible.

Somatic therapy

Somatic therapy is body-oriented psychotherapy, energetic attunement, and spiritual development based on Wilhelm Reich's work through the lens of Steven Kessler.

The work brings conscious awareness to how we block our life energy by perpetuating defense patterns adapted in childhood which keep us limited and disempowered.

My approach includes the body and its long-held defenses, what Reich called the body armor, alongside the mind.

This work is an integrative practice unifying mind, body, and spirit.

Together, we work toward a soothed nervous system, safety in the body, and presence instead of survival.

Integration sessions

I offer integration sessions to help you reflect on psychedelic experiences and bring what you learn into your personal growth. A moment of clarity is beautiful, but on its own, it is simply just a moment.

Please note: I offer integration support only. I do not provide, source, recommend or supervise any substance use. These substances remain illegal in New York outside approved settings.

Astrology

Each of us is born with inherited challenges that teach us about who we are and what we’re made of as we go through life. These are reflected in our natal charts.

My approach offers a way of developing awareness and understanding for our parents and ancestors and their dilemmas as well as for ourselves, as these patterns continue in our own lives until we become conscious of how to work with them. Many people find this understanding brings relief and compassion.

This is an optional layer that will deepen our sessions together.
